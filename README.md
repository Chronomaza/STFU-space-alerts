# STFU-space-alerts
Very simple mod that mutes those annoying low health/low energy alerts in space stage

Very strange thing I found out while making this mod: If the alert sounds are true absolute silence, the game instantly crashes upon them being played. 

Because of this the audio used for this mod is instead an incredibly quiet white noise that's not audible over the normal (already really quiet) space ambience. The alert sounds are also forced to be incredibly short loops of whatever their sound file is, meaning my custom made subtler space alert sounds that I spent over 30 minutes on played extremely weirdly, with my low energy sound effect not playing at all and the low health sound effect only playing in full after I healed- meaning there's some black box part of spore's code who's soul purpose is to loop/play these sound effects in highly spesific ways rather than just playing a short alarm sound on a loop- and the low health sound may always be playing with a silent part of it simply being looped instead. 

Conclusion?: The low health/energy alerts may be the most pointlessly overdeveloped part of this game. If I ever figure out how to implement them without crashing the game or having them bugged, I'll update this mod with an option for my more subtle alerts
